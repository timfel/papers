----------------------- REVIEW 3 ---------------------
PAPER: 3
TITLE: Squeak Makes a Good Python Debugger
AUTHORS: Fabio Niephaus, Tim Felgentreff, Tobias Pape and Robert Hirschfeld

Overall evaluation: 2

----------- Overall evaluation -----------
This paper discusses an exciting idea of hosting the execution of other programming languages in the Squeak environment, opening up the potential of rich liveness support to the target languages.

Technical details are provided with clear explanations, which would help the readers to implement a new language of their choice to be integrated into the Squeak environment. Related work compares the approach against prior effort, highlighting the originality of this work.

While the current form of the work is limited to the debugger, I am looking forward to the future work that covers the entire programming experience for Python (or other languages).

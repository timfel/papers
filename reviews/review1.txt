----------------------- REVIEW 1 ---------------------
PAPER: 2
TITLE: GraalSqueak: A Fast Smalltalk Bytecode Interpreter Written in an AST Interpreter Framework
AUTHORS: Fabio Niephaus, Tim Felgentreff and Robert Hirschfeld

Overall evaluation: 2 (accept)

----------- Overall evaluation -----------
This paper reports on implementing an AST-based and a bytecode-based Smalltalk versions on top of Truffle/Graal. The paper emphasizes the hints it had to construct in order to speed up the implementations. The paper provides an evaluation on micro benchmarks.

The AST-based implementation still operates at the traditional bytecode-level of Smalltalk implementation. For performance reason, this implementation then reconstructs higher-level ideas, such as loop nodes.

For the bytecode-based implementation, the trade-off is now that the implementation still needs to use the AST approach of Truffle. So the paper describes optimizations related to this mismatch.

Pros:
- The paper is well written.
- The paper is informative and insightful for anyone interested in using Truffle. It shares good tips, that could be useful in other scenarios, as well as being just an interesting case studies.

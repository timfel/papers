----------------------- REVIEW 2 ---------------------
PAPER: 2
TITLE: GraalSqueak: A Fast Smalltalk Bytecode Interpreter Written in an AST Interpreter Framework
AUTHORS: Fabio Niephaus, Tim Felgentreff and Robert Hirschfeld

Overall evaluation: 2 (accept)

----------- Overall evaluation -----------
This paper presents an implementation of Squeak/Smalltalk by using the
Truffle interpreter framework for GraalVM.  Unlike SOMns, which is
also based on Truffle but interprets an AST (abstract syntax tree) as
Truffle is designed for interpreting an AST, the presented
implementation can execute a traditional Smalltalk VM image containing
bytecode.  To do this, Smalltalk source code is first parsed and
compiled into bytecode by the Squeak compiler in the Smalltalk
environment.  The bytecode is passed to the Truffle interpreter,
decompiled to an AST by the authors' decompiler written in Java,
and executed by the authors' AST interpreter based on Truffle and
the Graal VM.  The authors also implemented a bytecode interpreter
using Truffle.  The paper presents the results of two micro benchmarks
and compares with the performance of other Smalltalk VMs.

Although this paper does not present a new implementation technique,
the reported result is interesting; the paper presents an interesting
aspect of the effectiveness of the Truffle framework.  Although the way
of using Truffle in this paper is somewhat different from the original
usage intended by the designers of Truffle, it is a sufficiently practical
problem setting because of backward compatibility.
